HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • ← / → or A / D — switch lane
  • ↑ / W / Space — jump (hold nothing, timing is everything)
  • ↓ / S — roll under signs and beams
  • Shift or double-tap the screen — deploy hoverboard
  • P / Esc — pause
  • Swipe anywhere on touch screens

PICKUPS

  • Coin Magnet — vacuums coins from all lanes
  • Jetpack — soar above the track on a coin stream
  • Score Multiplier — stacks with your combo
  • Super Sneakers — sky-high double jumps
  • Bubble Shield — eats one crash

TRICKS & SCORING

  • Ride the roofs of trains for a rooftop bonus every second.
  • Near-misses build a combo; the longer the chain, the fatter the multiplier.
  • Coin runs on train roofs are worth double — go up there.
  • Hoverboards absorb one wipe-out and give 20s of drift.
  • Every 500 m the world speeds up and swaps biome.
